<InShort>
- **Holodeck** gives repeatable, isolated, nested environments with built-in DNS, NTP, BGP and DHCP. It is best for teams and training.
- **Nested automation scripts** deploy a VCF or VVF fleet onto an existing vSphere host in about half an hour. They are best for individuals who rebuild often.
- **A physical "VCF in a box"** on one or two capable hosts is closest to real hardware behaviour. It is best for testing features like vSAN ESA, NSX edges or the Supervisor.
- **Hosted hands-on labs** cost nothing and need no setup, but you can't break or keep them.

All but the last need binaries and an entitlement, such as a trial, NFR, or VMUG Advantage with VCP-VCF.
</InShort>

## Match the lab to the goal

| Goal | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Learn the 9.x fleet model and workflows | Hosted labs first, then Holodeck |
| Rehearse a customer's 5.2 to 9.x upgrade | Nested lab built at the customer's starting version |
| Practise troubleshooting | Nested lab you can snapshot and break. See [break-fix exercises](/vcf/library/labs/break-fix-exercises/). |
| Test vSAN ESA, NVMe tiering or edge performance | Physical box |
| Develop automation | Any lab, plus API access. See [PowerCLI and the APIs](/vcf/library/labs/powercli-and-apis/). |
| Teach a team | Holodeck: one standard environment per student |

## Entitlement and binaries

You need an entitlement to download VCF 9 binaries and, in 9.0, a download token for the depot. VMUG Advantage members who hold the VCP-VCF certification are entitled to non-commercial licenses. Trials and partner NFR licenses are the other routes. Everything also runs in **90-day evaluation** after deployment, which is enough for a short project. Plan the depot early: see [an offline depot for your lab](/vcf/library/upgrades/getting-binaries/).

<Callout type="field">
Keep one long-lived lab at the version your estates run, and build disposable ones for the version they're moving to. Upgrade rehearsals are only useful when the starting point matches.
</Callout>

## Pitfalls

- **Under-sizing memory.** Size it first. See [sizing a VCF 9 lab](/vcf/library/labs/sizing-a-vcf-9-lab/).
- **Treating lab workarounds as production advice.** Label them clearly. See [ESX settings for labs](/vcf/library/labs/esx-settings-for-labs/).
- **Building a lab nobody can rebuild.** Script it, or use a toolkit that does.
