<InShort>
With **simple (single-node) deployment modes**, one instance of each 9.0 component adds up to about **48 vCPU, 194 GB of memory and 3.2 TB provisioned**, of which about 330 GB is actually used. **VCF Automation** is the constraint: a 24 vCPU, 96 GB VM, which needs a physical host with at least **12 cores and 24 threads**. Its memory can't be reduced. A fuller nested fleet with a four-node management domain and a three-node workload domain, as Holodeck builds it, needs about **30 cores, 325 GB of memory and 1.1 TB of disk**. Memory runs out first, so look at **NVMe memory tiering** before buying more RAM.
</InShort>

## The minimal 9.0 footprint

| Component | vCPU | Memory | Provisioned | Used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| vCenter | 4 | 21 GB | 642 GB | ~30 GB |
| NSX Manager | 6 | 24 GB | 300 GB | ~14 GB |
| SDDC Manager | 4 | 16 GB | 931 GB | ~77 GB |
| VCF Operations | 4 | 16 GB | 290 GB | ~13 GB |
| Fleet management (9.0) | 4 | 12 GB | 206 GB | ~117 GB |
| Collector | 4 | 16 GB | 280 GB | ~11 GB |
| VCF Automation | 24 | 96 GB | 626 GB | ~72 GB |

Figures from William Lam's measurements on 9.0. In 9.1, fleet management is replaced by the services runtime, which has its own node count and sizing, so measure your own build.

## What to buy, in order

1. **Memory** first, or NVMe capacity for memory tiering.
2. **Cores**: at least 12 cores and 24 threads if VCF Automation is in scope. Many 8-core consumer systems won't run it.
3. **NVMe storage**: thin provisioning hides most of the 3.2 TB, but vSAN ESA and snapshots add up.
4. **10 GbE** if you go physical with more than one host.

<Callout type="field">
Leave VCF Automation out of the first build unless the lab is for Automation. Deploy it later as a Day-N step. The rest of the fleet fits in much smaller hardware, and you learn the core platform faster.
</Callout>

## Pitfalls

- **Counting provisioned storage as used.** Thin disks make the real number much smaller, until snapshots pile up.
- **Hand-shrinking VCF Automation's vCPU.** It has been done, but behaviour beyond setup is not guaranteed.
- **Ignoring host memory overhead** for nested ESX VMs.
