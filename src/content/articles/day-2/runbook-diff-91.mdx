<InShort>
**Edit in three batches: the lines go false at different steps.** VCF Operations is step 1 of [Broadcom's upgrade order](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/deployment/upgrading-cloud-foundation.html), Log Management step 12, and vCenter step 17 from 9.0 or step 16 from 5.2. Workload domains can follow "at any time", so port 514 and vCLS lines stay true for some vCenters and false for others, and both versions have to stay in the runbook until the last one moves. Two jobs come before step 1: export from any Network Share plugin, because the upgrade deletes its data, and move every script off vCenter credentials.
</InShort>

This page holds only the lines to edit, ordered by the step that turns each one false. Broadcom's [support notes](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/release-notes/vmware-cloud-foundation-9-1-0-0-release-notes/vcf-91-product-support-notes.html) (5 Nov 2026) sort by product, which doesn't give that order <AsOf date="2026-11-07" />. The [deprecation radar](/vcf/library/architecture/deprecation-radar/) holds the full list by who has to act.

<Callout type="analysis" title="What is sourced here and what isn't">
Old lines are constructed examples, so match them to your own. New wording and quoted effects come from the linked pages. Where a source states no consequence, the "If nobody edits it" cell is inference. Every "Confirm" check is a suggestion that hasn't been run here, so try it outside production first.
</Callout>

## Which source to follow

On 9.1, follow the newer page on who owns passwords. The 9.1 [passwords page](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/fleet-management/manage-passwords.html), dated 5 November 2026, prefers VCF Operations. <KB id={435599} /> covers "VCF 9.x", was last modified on 13 April 2026 and calls SDDC Manager's Password Management "the authoritative source for account lifecycles".

On whether DRS needs vCLS, plan on the vCenter section of the 9.1 notes, as the radar does. <KB id={316514} /> lists only vCenter 7.0.x and 8.0.x and still warns that DRS "will not function" in Retreat Mode, but its note says vCLS can be disabled from vSphere 9.0 without affecting DRS or HA. The Installer section of the 9.1 notes agrees on decoupling, then says vCLS "will be deprecated in a future release".

Q41 of the [9.1 FAQ](https://www.vmware.com/docs/vmware-cloud-foundation-9-1-general-faqs) (4 Nov 2026) introduces Security Posture Management and calls 9.1 "upcoming". It doesn't mention syslog, vCLS or the login change, so it can't settle those lines.
