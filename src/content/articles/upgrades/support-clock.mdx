## Broadcom's dates against its 27-month support model

General support ends 17 Sep 2027 for 9.0, 11 Oct 2027 for vSphere 8 and "the VCF 5.x stack", and "August 2028" for 9.1.1, according to Broadcom's [13 Oct 2026 post](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2026/10/13/customer-favorite-capabilities-accelerating-your-path-to-vcf-9-1-1/). That post argues for an upgrade Broadcom wants you to make, so test its dates against the [July 2025 support model](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2025/07/16/vmware-cloud-foundation-9-ushers-in-new-support-model-and-release-cadence/), which expects 27 months for each initial minor release. Counted from the 9.0 or 9.1.0 GA, the sum fits Broadcom's date. Counted from the 9.1.1 GA, it doesn't:

| Sum | Result | Against Broadcom's statement |
|---|---|---|
| 9.0 GA 17 Jun 2025 + 27 months | 17 Sep 2027 | Same day |
| 9.1.0 GA 12 May 2026 + 27 months | 12 Aug 2028 | Same month; Broadcom gives no day |
| 9.1.1 GA 3 Sep 2026 + 27 months | 3 Dec 2028 | Doesn't fit "August 2028" |
| 17 Sep to 11 Oct 2027 | 24 days | "A few weeks earlier" |
| 11 Oct 2027 to 12 Aug 2028 | 306 days | Derived |

<Callout type="analysis" title="A maintenance release appears not to restart the clock">
On the reading the third row suggests, 9.1.1 inherits the date of 9.1.0, and waiting for a later 9.1.x gains no support time. That's an inference from the sums, and Broadcom doesn't state the rule. The day 12 Aug 2028 is derived too, though the tracker shows it and KB 456708 gives Cloud Director 10.6.2 the same day.
</Callout>

## What would change the answer

- **The portal disagrees.** Confirm each date in Broadcom's [Product Lifecycle portal](https://support.broadcom.com/web/ecx/productlifecycle) before you commit a roadmap. None has been checked against the portal, which refused automated requests on 6 Nov 2026. To check a whole inventory, [William Lam's January 2026 PowerShell function](https://williamlam.com/2026/01/programmatically-interact-with-the-broadcom-product-lifecycle-portal.html) queries the portal, and it can filter to items ending within 6 or 12 months.
- **The next release gets its own clock.** If the release Broadcom [plans](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2026/11/04/broadcom-vcf-portfolio-updates/) for "sometime in Q1FY27" is a new minor release and your migration hasn't started, compare it with 9.1.1 then. Broadcom doesn't name it yet, and that quarter runs to the start of February on [StorageReview's](https://www.storagereview.com/news/broadcom-vcf-portfolio-update-vsphere-express-live-recovery) reading. This page is due for review on 15 Feb 2027.
- **You buy Extended Support.** The [July 2025 post](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2025/07/16/vmware-cloud-foundation-9-ushers-in-new-support-model-and-release-cadence/) offers it for up to one year but doesn't say how that applies to each minor release, so ask Broadcom in writing. The EEA offer in the table below is separate and names only vSphere Standard 8 and Enterprise Plus 8.

## Other conflicts between sources

- **9.1.0 release date.** This page uses 12 May, from the 9.1.0 notes and KB 314608, while the [upgrade guide](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/deployment/upgrading-cloud-foundation.html) says 11 May 2026. The one-day difference doesn't change any deadline month.
- **5.2.1.0 release date.** This page uses 9 Oct 2024, which the 5.2.1 notes give for build 24307856. KB 314608 repeats 23 Jul 2024 from 5.2.0.0 for that build. The conflict can reach an upgrade plan: the forward-only upgrade rule compares release dates, so check a target against the VMware Interoperability Matrix, as the guide directs.
- **A KB that still points at 9.0.** KB 422643 recommends "the latest release of VCF Operations 9.0". With 9.0 ending on 17 Sep 2027 in the 13 Oct post, that isn't a reason to choose 9.0 as your target.
- **Tracker limits.** Don't plan on the Technical Guidance phase endoflife.date shows for 5.0 and 5.1, to 1 Jun 2028, because Broadcom's public pages don't confirm it (checked 6 Nov 2026). The [policy page it links](https://ftpdocs.broadcom.com/WebInterface/phpdocs/0/MSPSaccount/COMPAT/AllProdDates.HTML) is Broadcom's mainframe lifecycle page and has no VCF rows, so it can't back the tracker's dates, and the tracker's 9.x dates match the sums above and add no separate evidence. The tracker also lists 5.2.3 as the latest 5.2, while Broadcom has 5.2.4.
