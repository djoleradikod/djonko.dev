<InShort>
**Target 9.1.1 from every state below.** Stepping to 9.0 ends general support on 17 Sep 2027, 24 days before vSphere 8 and VCF 5.2.x. 9.1.0 refuses upgrades from 5.2.4, 8.0 U3j and U3k. Find your row, then make the plan in the tool it names. <AsOf date="2026-11-06" />
</InShort>

| You run | Target | Route | What blocks it | Support date you land on | Next |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VCF 5.0 or 5.1 | 9.1.1, through 5.2.x | Two hops | Management domain and all workload domains must reach 5.2.x first | Unresolved until you move: 1 Jun or 11 Oct 2027, so plan to June. Then August 2028 | [Support clock](/vcf/library/upgrades/support-clock/). The Planner starts at 5.2, so use it after the first hop |
| VCF 5.2.0 to 5.2.3 | 9.1.1 | Skip-level, VCF Operations first | vIDM, Aria Operations for Logs and Aria Suite Lifecycle have no upgrade to 9.1 | August 2028 | Planner, VCF 5.2. [Planning note](/vcf/library/upgrades/planning-52-to-90/) |
| VCF 5.2.4 | 9.1.1 only | As above | 9.1.0 is older than 5.2.4 | August 2028 | As above |
| VCF 9.0.x | 9.1.1 | Upgrade, VCF Operations first | Lam's 3 Sep table closes 9.0.2 EP2 to 9.1.0. Identity Broker appliance off the management network: redeploy it first | August 2028. Staying: 17 Sep 2027 | Planner, VCF 9.0. [Management services note](/vcf/library/upgrades/90-to-91-management-services/) |
| VCF 9.1.0.x | 9.1.1 | Patch, fleet lifecycle first | No Upgrade All until the 9.1.1 dependencies are met | August 2028, unchanged by this site's reading | [Upgrade order](/vcf/library/upgrades/upgrade-order-changed/) |
| vSphere 8.0 U3a to U3i, no fleet | VCF 9.1.1 | Converge | ELM, baselines, a vCenter already connected to an SDDC Manager. Aria Operations 8.18.7 goes only to 9.1.1 or later | August 2028 once all of it is on 9.1.x | [Converge or import](/vcf/library/upgrades/converge-import-vsphere/), then Planner |
| vSphere 8.0 U3j or U3k | VCF 9.1.1 | Upgrade or converge | 9.1.0 allows import only. Converge from these builds is implied, not stated, so check the Matrix | As above | Matrix, then Planner |
| vSphere 8, a fleet at 9.0 or later | Workload domain | Import | vCenter below 8.0 U3a, ESX below 8.0 U3, NSX below 4.2.1. No partial import | 11 Oct 2027 until the domain is upgraded | [Converge or import](/vcf/library/upgrades/converge-import-vsphere/) |
| vSphere 8, no NSX or Aria Automation, no fleet wanted | vSphere or VVF 9.1.x | Component update, or VCF Installer for VVF | NSX or Aria Automation removes the component option. VVF 9.1 with management services can't later converge to VCF 9.1 | August 2028 | Planner's two VVF paths |
| vSphere 8 below 8.0 U3a | Then as above | Patch first | Pick a build with a path to your target | 11 Oct 2027 until you move | Matrix |

The blockers and routes come from the [upgrade guide](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/deployment/upgrading-cloud-foundation.html) and [paths page](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/deployment/overview-of-deploy--converge--and-upgrade.html) (both updated 5 Nov 2026), the [converge configurations page](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vcf/vcf-9-0-and-later/9-1/deployment/converging-your-existing-vsphere-infrastructure-to-a-vcf-or-vvf-platform-/supported-and-not-supported-configurations.html), <KB id={440630} />, <KB id={448135} /> and <KB id={455088} />, with 9.0.2 EP2 from [Lam's 3 Sep post](https://williamlam.com/2026/09/10-exciting-enhancements-in-vmware-cloud-foundation-9-1-1.html). Broadcom's [13 Oct post](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2026/10/13/customer-favorite-capabilities-accelerating-your-path-to-vcf-9-1-1/) gives the support dates. It's arguing for the upgrade, and the [support clock](/vcf/library/upgrades/support-clock/) checks its arithmetic. If you hold perpetual licences, [FAQ Q27](https://www.vmware.com/docs/vmware-cloud-foundation-9-1-general-faqs) says you must switch to subscription before upgrading to VCF 9; ask Broadcom about your own contract.

## What the table doesn't show

**The landing date belongs to each component.** The guide calls workload domain upgrades optional, to run any time after the management domain. <KB id={456708} /> still ends VCF 5.2.x, ESXi 8.x, vCenter 8.x and NSX 4.x on 11 Oct 2027.

<Callout type="analysis" title="Inference: count the date from what you leave behind">
A management domain on 9.1.1 with one workload domain on 5.2 has an October 2027 date for that domain. An imported vSphere 8 domain has the same date until it moves. When you report a date, give the one for the last domain still to move.
</Callout>

**Patching the source can close the route.** Upgrades only go to a release newer than your build, and the guide allows a patch target "only if you can also upgrade to its parent maintenance release". The 13 Oct post warns that a future out-of-band patch could bring a "brand-new back-in-time restriction".

<Callout type="analysis" title="Inference: 9.1.1.0 sets the cutoff">
By those two rules, a source build released after 3 Sep 2026 has no path to 9.1.1, even though 9.1.1 patches dated 12 Oct exist. It would wait for a newer maintenance release. Before applying a vSphere 8, NSX 4 or 5.2 patch during the project, compare its release date with 3 Sep 2026 and check the pair in the Matrix.
</Callout>

**Multi-instance estates choose the fleet first.** [Lam's relocation table](https://williamlam.com/2026/07/vcf-9-1-vcf-instance-fleet-consolidation-relocation.html) (14 Jul, updated 1 Aug 2026) supports moving 5.x and 9.0.x instances into a 9.1 fleet before they upgrade, and marks moving an existing 9.1 instance as unsupported. So consolidation is a decision to make before each instance's upgrade. The upgrade guide, paths page and FAQ don't cover it, so this rests on his post, and he says later support is under evaluation.

**Waiting for the next release needs a reason.** Broadcom [plans it](https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2026/11/04/broadcom-vcf-portfolio-updates/) for "sometime in Q1FY27" without naming it, and that post gives no support dates for it. Plan to 9.1.1 now, and compare once it has a name and notes.

## Which tool, and when

- **[Lam's path map](https://williamlam.com/2026/05/vcf-9-1-demystifying-supported-upgrade-paths-to-9-1.html) (23 May 2026).** Use it to show stakeholders what each end state adds. It works in version families and predates 9.1.1, so don't take a source build from it.
- **[VCF Upgrade Planner](https://vmware.github.io/vcf-upgrade-planner/), described in [Lam's post of 22 Oct 2026](https://williamlam.com/2026/10/major-enhancements-to-vcf-9-1-upgrade-planner-tool.html).** Use it once the row above is settled, to get the ordered steps with your exact builds, including workload domain and instance paths, Express Patches, and Supervisor and VKS. It exports for change boards. The guide and the 9.1.1 notes both point to it. Its [repository](https://github.com/vmware/vcf-upgrade-planner) changed as late as 5 Nov. Its refresh script's own header says the compatibility check is done by linking to Broadcom's site, so treat a green result as a prompt to confirm.
- **[Interoperability Matrix](https://interopmatrix.broadcom.com/Upgrade?productId=851).** Use it last, for every build pair in the plan. <KB id={448135} /> says to uncheck "Hide Patch Releases" when searching.
- **<KB id={450972} />.** Use it only to explain why a 9.1.0 target failed. It doesn't list 9.1.1.
