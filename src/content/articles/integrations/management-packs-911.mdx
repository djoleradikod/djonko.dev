<InShort>
Before upgrading VCF Operations to **9.1.1**, list your installed management packs. Sixteen of them, including the database packs, ServiceNow, SNMP and network devices, Kubernetes, HCX, Protection and Recovery, Live Recovery and both Avi packs, **stop collecting until upgraded to 9.1.1** (<KB id={454432} />). The upgrade precheck flags them. Packs built with MP Builder must be re-saved and re-installed from the in-product builder (<KB id={454434} />). And from 9.1: no Logs content packs inside VCF Operations, no standalone MP Builder appliance, and no direct vCenter authentication for VCF Operations.
</InShort>

## Packs that must move to 9.1.1

| Group | Packs |
|---|---|
| Databases | Microsoft SQL Server, MongoDB, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle |
| Platforms and tools | ServiceNow, Network Devices, SNMP, Orchestrator, Kubernetes, Aggregator |
| VMware products | VCF Operations HCX, Protection and Recovery, VMware Live Recovery cloud |
| Load balancing | Avi LB Observability, Avi LB Operations |

## A sequence that avoids gaps

1. **Inventory**: export the list from Integrations > Repository, with versions.
2. **Download** the 9.1.1 versions from the Solutions Catalog before the window.
3. **Upgrade VCF Operations**, then the packs straight away in the same window.
4. **Rebuild custom packs** made with MP Builder in the in-product builder, then re-install.
5. **Check** each account returns to Collecting and Data receiving.

## HCX on 9.0

No HCX management pack supported VCF Operations 9.0.x, and pack 5.4.0 is incompatible with it (<KB id={437447} />). On 9.1.1, use the VCF Operations HCX pack listed above.

<Callout type="field">
Treat management packs as part of the platform bill of materials, with an owner per pack. Without a list of what is installed, a change like the one in 9.1.1 leaves you no way to tell which dashboards have gone quiet.
</Callout>

## Pitfalls

- **Upgrading VCF Operations alone** and finding empty dashboards the next morning.
- **Forgetting partner packs** that aren't on the list but have their own compatibility notes.
- **Keeping the standalone MP Builder** in the estate. It is no longer maintained.
