<InShort>
Read health in this order: **proxy** (Online, Offline, Going Online), **account status** (Collecting, Warning, Failed), the **tooltip** on a warning (State and Status), and the **admin UI** collection status (Data receiving, No data receiving). The combination usually tells you the fix. **Proxy Online, account Collecting, status Object down** means the credentials are wrong. **State: Stopping** that never finishes means a stuck collector: stop and start collection. **PKIX** or **SSLHandshake** errors mean certificate trust. **Duplicate key ... CertificateConfig** means a duplicate certificate in a chain or in trusted certificates (<KB id={449013} />).
</InShort>

## The combinations

| Proxy | Account | Detail | Meaning and fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online | Collecting | Status: Object down | Credentials wrong or rotated. Rotate or update, validate, save (<KB id={421958} />). |
| Online | Warning | State: Stopping | Collector stuck. Stop collecting, then start (<KB id={429451} />). |
| Online | Failed | "Certificate verification failed" | The endpoint certificate changed. Edit, validate, accept the new certificate (<KB id={325773} />). |
| Online | Failed | PKIX or SSLHandshakeException | Trust chain. Import leaf, intermediate and root into Trusted Certificates (<KB id={421016} />). |
| Online | Warning | "Unable to connect to VC: Duplicate key" | Duplicate certificate in vCenter's chain. Rebuild the chain (<KB id={449013} />). |
| Offline or Going Online | Any | | Fix the proxy first: DNS, outbound 443, registration. "Going Online" can take 20 minutes after first boot. |

## Two cases that look broken but aren't

- **"No data receiving" on vCenter from the Supervisor adapter** is by design. It only borrows vCenter's credentials (<KB id={410383} />).
- **Duplicate VCF adapters**, one by IP and one by FQDN, cause "Service or view is not available". Delete one **without** deleting related objects (<KB id={433259} />).

## Where the logs are

```text
On the node or proxy running the adapter:
  /storage/log/vcops/log/collector.log
  /storage/log/vcops/log/adapters/VMwareAdapter/      vCenter
  /storage/log/vcops/log/adapters/VcfAdapter/          VCF account
  /storage/log/vcops/log/adapters/ManagementAdapter/   passwords, certificates, licensing tasks
On a cloud proxy:
  cprc-cli -s                                           proxy status
```

If "Invalid username or password" persists with system-managed credentials, the analytics log may show that service account creation failed. Rotate, then validate (<KB id={446874} />).

<Callout type="field">
Teach the four-word rule to the operations team: "Online, Collecting, Object down: credentials." It is the single most common integration ticket, and it never needs escalation.
</Callout>

## Pitfalls

- **Deleting and re-adding an account** as the first move. You lose history and sometimes create duplicates.
- **Deleting related objects** when removing a duplicate adapter.
- **Reading logs on the wrong node.** Adapters run where their collector is.
